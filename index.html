The benchmark

Assembly reasoning beyond isolated 3D objects.

Recent vision-language models (VLMs) have demonstrated strong capabilities in robotic spatial-relation reasoning, object manipulation, environmental perception, and task planning, yet their ability to reason about complex robotic component assemblies remains largely unexplored. OmniCAD introduces a large-scale benchmark for assembly-aware 3D spatial reasoning across diverse industrial mechanical systems. The benchmark contains 25K industrial mechanical assemblies with component parts, 3D poses, mate relations, multi-view renderings, and structured assembly annotations. It evaluates component-level 3D spatial reasoning, part-to-part relational reasoning, and tool-augmented agentic assembly reasoning.

Each sample is derived from a native SolidWorks assembly and its referenced parts. Structured annotations include component instances, library mappings, mate relationships, directed assembly graphs, spatial transformations, component meshes, assembly meshes, rendered reference views, and mate graphs.

Three evaluation tracks

From component inventory to physically valid assembly.

OmniCAD separates geometric placement, relational assembly structure, and iterative agentic correction so that each source of reasoning failure can be measured independently.

01

Component-level 3D spatial reasoning

Infer which component instances are required and estimate the 3D position and orientation of every part from multimodal assembly evidence.

02

Part-to-part relational reasoning

Recover component-level mate relationships, mate types, and the assembly connectivity structure, including repeated library reuse.

03

Tool-augmented agentic reasoning

Iteratively revise assembly predictions using 2D visualization, mate-graph priors, and geometric conflict feedback generated from the current prediction.

Agentic framework

Predict, inspect, and revise.

At each round, the model receives the original inputs, its previous structured prediction, and the latest tool feedback. Runs terminate when the model stops requesting feedback or after ten rounds.

2DVisClean rendering of the current prediction with requested global view rotation and scale.
MG+2DVisAdds the component-level mate graph as a structural prior.
Conflict+2DVisAdds AABB, OBB, and mesh-interpenetration diagnostics for potentially conflicting component pairs.
